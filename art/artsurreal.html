How to use this studio
1. Keep the style foundation stable as your visual signature.
2. Change one or two visual systems at a time (for example linework + palette) so you can compare results.
3. Use the scene field for the image idea, not for repeating the style.
4. Save/export settings when you find a combination worth reusing.
5. Treat the generated prompt as a structured starting point; remove contradictory phrases before sending it to a model.
