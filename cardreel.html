Card Reel

Card: portrait, about 600 × 1030 px (or 900 × 1545 for sharper). Any ratio works, the 3D card matches it. Crop off white borders.

Card back: same proportions as the front, about 600 × 1030 px. It is cropped to fit the front's shape. It shows during the opening flip and whenever the card turns away.

Background: exactly 1080 × 1920 px (9:16), JPG or PNG. Other sizes are cropped to fill. The card sits in the middle and text covers the bottom 40%, so keep detail to the edges; a dark fade is added behind the text.

Records a 1080×1920 vertical reel, ready for Instagram.
