Pattern Reference Guide
Grand Trine
Three planets, each 120° apart, forming an equilateral triangle. Talent, ease, flow — but can indicate complacency if not activated.
T-Square
Two planets in opposition (180°), both square (90°) to a third apex planet. High tension, great drive. The apex planet is the focal point of challenge.
Grand Cross
Four planets in two oppositions, all square each other. Maximum tension across all axes. Rare, intense, demands mastery of four life areas.
Yod (Finger of God)
Two planets sextile (60°) each other, both quincunx (150°) a third apex. A point of fate, adjustment, spiritual mission. The apex planet is redirected repeatedly.
Stellium
Three or more planets in the same sign or house (within 8°). Concentrated energy, strong focus, dominant theme in the chart.
Kite
Grand Trine with a fourth planet opposing one trine planet and sextiling the other two. The kite gives the trine a point of tension and release — making the ease productive.
