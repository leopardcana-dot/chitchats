How to use this
  1. Search or filter — type in the box or tap a category chip.
  2. Set the amount — each food shows kcal, protein, carbs, sugar, and fat per 100g. Type your grams in the small box (a typical serving is pre-filled).
  3. Pick a meal — Breakfast / Lunch / Dinner / Snack.
  4. Add it — tap the dark + button. Everything recalculates for your amount.
  5. Adjust anytime — change grams in the log, or tap × to remove.
  6. Set goals — enter kcal and macro targets (P/C/S/F) to see progress rings.
  7. Add your own — enter any item's per-100g values including sugar.
  8. Summary tab — totals, macro split, sugar note, and meal-by-meal breakdown.
Sugar is listed separately from total carbs (sugar is a subset of carbs). Values run in your browser and persist via localStorage.

Data sources

  • USDA FoodData Central — primary reference
  • Italian INRAN / CREA — grains and pasta
  • Croatian & Serbian food composition data — regional items
  • French Ciqual — dairy
  • Regional Balkan studies — traditional dishes

Sugar values are total sugars (natural + added). Same food can vary 5–30% between countries. For calorie counting, consistency (raw vs cooked) matters more than country.
